## Destination and eligibility review
- Ghana: Ghana Immigration Service lists Nigeria/ECOWAS passports as visa-exempt: https://gis.gov.gh/visas/
- Kenya: Kenya 2025 amended eTA exemption lists Nigerian nationals for stays up to 60 days. Verify each passport and any airline/health requirements: https://evisa.go.ke/general-information
- Tanzania: Nigerian nationals removed from additional referral visa category effective September 2026, although some older tourism pages retain out-of-date listings. Use embassy/current immigration rules rather than older tourism FAQ: https://www.ng.tzembassy.go.tz/services/type-of-visa and https://www.thecable.ng/tanzania-removes-special-visa-clearance-requirement-for-nigerians/
- Zanzibar: mandatory Zanzibar inbound visitor insurance (ZIC), separate from ordinary travel insurance: https://tempweb.zic.co.tz/inbound-insurance
- UAE: visitor visa must be assessed and issued by appropriate UAE authority / authorised partners; no promise of approval: https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/tourist-visa
- Dubai Global Village: Season 31 runs from 14 Oct 2026 through May 2027: https://www.visitdubai.com/en/places-to-visit/global-village
- South Africa: Nigeria visitor visa applications/VFS guidance: https://visa.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/southafrica/nigeria/english/
- Cape Town Table Mountain Cableway: weather permitting with current official fare/booking policy: https://www.tablemountain.net/plan-your-visit/ticket-prices/

## Customer-facing design and pricing
- The public listing displays the five experiences in featured order. Starting NGN prices show "From" and "Per adult sharing · Lagos departure".
- Each detail page offers day-by-day experience plan, included components, separately paid extras, required documentation, and a personal enquiry link. Existing provider/booking services are not faked.
- No copied competitor marketing language or images. Until approved photos are uploaded, the official Sapphire Quest emblem appears on package cards; the shared package hero photo is used at detail level.
- No technical disclaimer "Displayed prices, if any, are indicative ..." is shown. Final trip details are confirmed before payment in natural customer-facing language.
- Legal page text has been polished without removing relevant consumer travel protections.
- Old demonstration records are hidden from public presentation by exact slugs; they are not deleted from the database.

## How staff edit the five packages and add photos
1. On the live site open /admin/packages after signing into the existing staff workspace.
2. Owner or manager clicks "Add five packages to editor" once; existing rows with the same slug are never overwritten.
3. Expand any package to edit its price (NGN), itinerary, highlights or photo gallery using the existing editor.
4. Photos can be uploaded through ImageKit if configured (max 5 per package, <5 MB) or added via approved HTTPS image links.
5. Save, refresh the public listing, confirm the edits appear through the database override.
6. To hide a specific featured package, set its status to draft in the staff editor; the catalogue respects that choice.

## Before accepting paid bookings
- Supplier-confirmed return airfare from Lagos for actual date range and baggage allowance; alternative origin price.
- Named hotel, property category, meal basis, room-sharing policy, infant/child rates and holiday supplements.
- Transport/tour partner availability, local site admission, service taxes, insurance and safety restrictions.
- Visa, yellow-fever, passport, insurance and travel-document validation by nationality and itinerary.
- Real margin/net cost, operator licence/agreements, cancellation/refund rules, payment instructions and customer consent.
- Written package confirmation before collection of payment. No fabricated inventory, airline reservations, countdowns, reviews or discounts.
